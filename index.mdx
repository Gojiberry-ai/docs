Gojiberry finds B2B leads that show buying signals, scores them with AI, and reaches out to them on LinkedIn and by email. The API and the MCP server give you the same data and actions as the app, so you can connect Gojiberry to your CRM, your own product, an automation tool, or an AI assistant.

With them you can:

- Read and update your contacts, their signals, and their scores.
- Create lists, source agents, and campaigns, and follow their results.
- Search the Directory and reveal new leads.
- Read and answer your LinkedIn conversations.
- Get notified in real time with webhooks.

## Base URL

**Production:** `https://ext.gojiberry.ai`

## Example usage

```bash
# Get all contacts
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
     https://ext.gojiberry.ai/v1/contact

# Get contacts with filtering
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
     "https://ext.gojiberry.ai/v1/contact?search=john&agent=1&dateFrom=2024-01-01"

# Get a specific contact
curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
     https://ext.gojiberry.ai/v1/contact/123
```

Authentication, impersonation, and rate limits are on the [Quickstart](/quickstart) page. Webhook delivery and signature verification are on the [Webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks) page.
