## Why sync both ways

Your CRM knows who your customers, open deals, and opt-outs are. Gojiberry knows who your agents found, who replied, and who a teammate rejected. When the two disagree, the same person gets two first messages, or a customer gets a cold invitation.

A two-way sync fixes that:

- **CRM to Gojiberry.** Push the contacts you want to work, and the actions they take in your product (trial signup, demo booked), as signals next to the ones Gojiberry detects.
- **Gojiberry to CRM.** Mark rejected leads in the CRM, with the reason, so no rep picks them up again.
- **Both ways.** Reject in Gojiberry anyone your sales team already works, so campaigns skip them.

Send `Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY`. The base URL is `https://ext.gojiberry.ai`. With the [MCP server](/mcp-server), your assistant runs the tools named in each step for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Push a CRM contact into Gojiberry">
    `POST /v1/contact` creates the contact. `firstName`, `lastName`, and `profileUrl` (the LinkedIn profile) are required. Pass `listId` to drop the contact straight into the list a campaign reaches out to.

    **MCP tool:** `create_contact`

    ```bash
    curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{
        "firstName": "Jane",
        "lastName": "Doe",
        "profileUrl": "https://www.linkedin.com/in/janedoe",
        "email": "jane@acme.com",
        "company": "Acme",
        "jobTitle": "Head of Sales",
        "listId": 321
      }' \
      "https://ext.gojiberry.ai/v1/contact"
    ```

    The response is the new contact. Store its `id` on the CRM record, in a field such as "Gojiberry ID": every following call needs it.

    When the person is already in your workspace, or is in your excluded contacts, no contact is created and the response is a status instead:

    ```json
    { "success": false, "messageCode": "CONTACT_ALREADY_EXISTS" }
    ```

    `CONTACT_ALREADY_EXCLUDED` means the person is excluded, for example because their contact was deleted earlier, and stays blocked. For `CONTACT_ALREADY_EXISTS`, find the existing contact with `GET /v1/contact?search=jane@acme.com` (the search matches name, email, company, and job title) and store its `id`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Push first-party signals">
    `POST /v1/contact/{id}/signals` adds what the lead did in your product or stack, next to the signals Gojiberry agents detect. Describe the event in `intent`, add a short label in `intentKeyword`, and leave `intentType` empty.

    **MCP tool:** none yet. Call the API from your CRM workflow, backend, Zapier, Make, or n8n.

    ```bash
    curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{ "intent": "Booked a demo from the pricing page", "intentKeyword": "demo_booked" }' \
      "https://ext.gojiberry.ai/v1/contact/90210/signals"
    ```

    The response is `{ "created": true, "contact": { ... } }`, with the contact's signals most recent first. A signal without `intentType` is recorded on every call, so send each event once: a retry adds a duplicate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep campaigns away from people sales already works">
    When a CRM contact becomes a customer, gets an open deal, or opts out, reject them in Gojiberry. `POST /v1/contact/{id}/reject` removes the contact from the review queue and from campaign scheduling. `contact_known_or_duplicate` is the reason for "already known".

    **MCP tool:** `reject_contact`

    ```bash
    curl -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{ "reason": "contact_known_or_duplicate" }' \
      "https://ext.gojiberry.ai/v1/contact/90210/reject"
    ```

    Other reason codes cover a wrong company size, industry, location, title, or seniority, and `other` takes a free-text `reasonText`. A rejection is reversible with `POST /v1/contact/{id}/unreject` (MCP tool `unreject_contact`), which puts the contact back in the review queue, not in a campaign.

    <Warning>
      For people who must never be imported again, `DELETE /v1/contact/{id}` removes the contact and adds them to your excluded contacts, so agents skip them and `POST /v1/contact` refuses them. Deleting cannot be undone.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mirror rejected leads back to the CRM">
    Rejections made in Gojiberry, by a teammate in the review queue or through the API, carry `rejectedAt`, `rejectedByUserId`, `rejectionReason`, and `rejectionReasonText` on the contact. Read them with `GET /v1/contact`, a list at a time with `listId`, and copy every contact whose `rejectedAt` is set onto its CRM record.

    **MCP tool:** `list_contacts`, or `get_contact` for one contact. Try asking: *"Which leads did my team reject this week, and why?"*

    ```bash
    curl -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
      "https://ext.gojiberry.ai/v1/contact?listId=321&page=1&limit=100"
    ```

    ```json
    {
      "contacts": [
        {
          "id": 90210,
          "fullName": "Jane Doe",
          "email": "jane@acme.com",
          "rejectedAt": "2026-10-13T09:12:44.000Z",
          "rejectedByUserId": 17,
          "rejectionReason": "company_wrong_size",
          "rejectionReasonText": null
        }
      ],
      "total": 240,
      "page": 1,
      "limit": 100,
      "totalPages": 3
    }
    ```

    Set the CRM record to "Disqualified" or "Do not contact", and keep the reason. Walk `page` through `totalPages`, and run the scan on a schedule, for example every night.

    <Note>
      The [webhook](/api-reference/webhooks) tells you when a contact is created or updated, which keeps the rest of the CRM record current. A rejection does not send it, so the scheduled scan is what catches rejections.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every field is listed on [Create a contact](/api-reference/contacts/create-a-contact), [Add a signal](/api-reference/contacts/add-a-signal), [Reject a lead](/api-reference/contacts/reject-a-lead), and [List contacts](/api-reference/contacts/list-contacts).
